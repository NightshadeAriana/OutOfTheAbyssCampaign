Events in this session were retconned. [[Players/Fallen Leaf\|Fallen Leaf]] and [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]] meet in [[Locations/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]].
# Game events
[[Players/Fallen Leaf\|Fallen Leaf]] is launched out of a magic circle in the middle of [[Locations/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]. Confused, she questions where she is. Before her stands a masked character, [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]], and a winged cat, [[NPCs/Other/Max\|Max]]. While [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]] hesitates to reveal any information, he at least reveals his name. A Tabaxi looks strange in this place. [[Players/Fallen Leaf\|Fallen Leaf]] does say she is from far away and doesn't recognize this place.

After a while, the two go into a tavern. [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]] offers [[Players/Fallen Leaf\|Fallen Leaf]] to stay at his room. In the tavern, [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]] sees a [[Quests/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Deliver Supplies to Phandalin\|job offer]] to escort a caravan to [[Locations/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Phandalin\|Phandalin]], and the two accept it.
