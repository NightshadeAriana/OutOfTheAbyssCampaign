Magnus is a highly secretive, masked magic-user who commands a winged cat familiar named [[NPCs/Other/Max\|Max]], utilizes a wide range of magic from trickery to summoning [[Assets/Stat Blocks/Lemure\|Lemures]], and frequently employs manipulation or deception to achieve his goals. After concluding the [[Modules/Lost Mine of Phandelver\|Lost Mine of Phandelver]] and being betrayed by [[NPCs/Charlotte\|Charlotte]], he recently escaped from drow enslavement in [[Locations/Out of the Abyss/Velkynvelve\|Velkynvelve]].

# Lore
## Appearance
For the longest time, Magnus hid his face. Height unknown, appearance unknown. After the party was enslaved, Magnus could no longer hide.

## Known details
Magnus is a spellcaster from [[Locations/Other/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]] who relies on a familiar named Max for scouting. In combat and exploration, he uses Fire Bolt as his most common spell, and has been seen casting a powerful spell that summons a small army of Lemures. He relies on trickery and manipulation, such as using Thaumaturgy to trick a goblin into stabbing itself, casting Distort Value to lower shop prices, and distracting an ogre with a Minor Illusion of meat. He actively takes magical items and loot, taking the [[Items/Wand of Decent Laughter\|Wand of Decent Laughter]], claiming a [[Items/Staff of Defense\|Staff of Defense]], and using Mage Hand to steal gold from [[NPCs/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Clera\|someone]]. He traded a necklace to Agatha in exchange for information regarding an ancient spellbook under Waterdeep. He is capable of speaking Infernal.
