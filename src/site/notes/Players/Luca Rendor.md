Luca Rendor is a former member of the adventuring party who participated in the initial journey toward [[Locations/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Phandalin\|Phandalin]]. After a brief stint with the group during their first major encounter, he left the party entirely.

# Details
## Appearance
Luca Rendor measures 3'6, he has about an inch and a half long hair, and wears leather clothes.

## Relationships
Traveled alongside [[Players/Fallen Leaf\|Fallen Leaf]], [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]], and [[Players/Tyr\|Tyr]] during his short time with the group.

## Known details
When the party met, Luca Rendor revealed that he was often wandering the streets, and used his instruments to get some money. As well as thievery.

## Out of game events
Luca Rendor quit the game before after [[Sessions/Session 1\|Session 1]].
