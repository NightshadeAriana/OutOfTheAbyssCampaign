Tyr is a formidable warrior whose intimidating presence often precedes him, having once terrified a barmaid who mistook him for an orc. He is a capable frontline fighter with a documented dislike for goblins, yet he possesses surprising cunning, using his knowledge of the Orcish language to deceive and manipulate enemy camps. After being poisoned and captured by [[NPCs/Charlotte\|Charlotte]] in [[Locations/Other/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]], he recently fought his way out of the drow slave pens of [[Locations/Out of the Abyss/Velkynvelve\|Velkynvelve]].

# Lore
## Appearance
Tyr appears to be large, intimidating Orc to those who have never seen one of his kind.



## Known details
Tyr is a physically strong character who is often tasked with breaking down doors and gates. He can understand and speak Orcish. In towns, his appearance has caused a terrified barmaid to mistake him for an orc, though he also attracted the stares of teenage women in a tavern. He engages in brutal combat tactics, such as tying up a goblin named Georgefoot to use as a meat shield, and killing two Ruffians with a slash of his shortsword. His weapons include a +1 Morningstar and a shortsword.
