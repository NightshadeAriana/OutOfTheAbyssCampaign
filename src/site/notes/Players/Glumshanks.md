Glumshanks is a goblin who used to work for the [[Groups/Redbrands\|Redbrands]] before joining the party. Kidnapped by [[NPCs/Charlotte\|Charlotte Mizzrym]], he escaped [[Locations/Out of the Abyss/Velkynvelve\|Velkynvelve]] with his party. He was charmed by a [[Assets/Stat Blocks/Succubus\|Succubus]] during the [[Sessions/Session 12\|assassination of the Deepking]] in Gracklstugh.

# Lore
## Appearance
Glunshanks is known to be smaller than 3'6. He has green skin and red hair. He is known to use a hand crossbow, a minc and a dagger.

## Known details
Glumshanks used to work for the Redbrands. He is obsessed with drinking all kinds of liquids [[Sessions/Session 4\|after being cursed by a potion of insatiable thirst]].

When he joined the party, he revealed he had been watching the party and was aware of a previous [[Sessions/Session 1\|goblin massacre]]. Before joining the party, he was secretly hired by Iarno Albrek to work alongside the Redbrands. He favors stealth and precision strikes, but doesn't shy refuse to get creative with his weapon usage, like dual-wielding hand crossbows, stabbing a drow in the throat with two daggers at once, and executing a Drow Elite Warrior with a crossbow bolt between the eyes. He has a highly unusual appetite after drinking a potion if insatiable thirst, drinking a bottle of blood, believing a deadly potion of sight rot looked delicious, and stealing rainwater from a homeless man's hat to drink. He accidentally drew hostile Shadows and Zombies to the party by playing his lute in Wave Echo Cave.
