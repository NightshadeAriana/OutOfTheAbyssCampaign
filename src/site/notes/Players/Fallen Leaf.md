Fallen Leaf is a magic-user with a strong affinity for nature and animals, frequently using spells like Spike Growth to control the battlefield. She also has a reckless streak that occasionally results in her being knocked unconscious during combat. Originally she met her companion [[Players/Magnus\|Magnus]] in [[Locations/Lost Mine of Phandelver/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] to take on a caravan job. She escaped drow enslavement in [[Locations/Out of the Abyss/Velkynvelve\|Velkynvelve]]. She was tricked by a [[Assets/Stat Blocks/Out of the Abyss/Skum\|Skum]] and killed by an [[Assets/Stat Blocks/Out of the Abyss/Aboleth\|Aboleth]].

## Known details
Fallen Leaf lacked formal combat training, which was reflected in her hesitation to strike lethally and her frequent knockouts. She is easily [[Sessions/Session 3\|distracted by rodents and felines]], and [[Sessions/Session 8\|confused by laser-like spells]]. She loves fish so much she [[Sessions/Session 5\|bought a fishing rod]]. She is easily frightened by the dangers of adventuring, particularly massive swarms and the treacherous environments they explore. Fallen Leaf [[Sessions/Session 7\|acts more agressively towards Drow]]. Fallen Leaf mentioned some friends who were enslaved by the Drow. She fears spiders.

She frequently takes on a healing and supportive role, healing others despite being close to death herself. She exhibits specific fears and dislikes, becoming frightened by spiders and hating the smell of zombies. She is perceptive to magic, successfully identifying a [[Items/Potion of Mage Armor\|Potion of Mage Armor]] and a disease-inducing potion of sight rot. She occasionally cooks for the party, buying cooking utensils and ingredients, and preparing a quick meal for the group during a short rest, while the party used to have time. But her cooking skills quickly became obsolete.

Leaf doesn't hesitate to [[Sessions/Session 12\|stand up for others]] if the situation is dire, often risking her own life. She risked her life trying to save nearly half of her party. She is naive, and her naivity got her [[Sessions/Session 12\|tricked and killed]].
